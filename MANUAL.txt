DATE CALCULATOR -- USER MANUAL
===============================

Welcome! This program turns your Agon Light 2 into a handy date
calculator, inspired by the classic HP-12C financial calculator.


GETTING STARTED
---------------

Copy the file datecalc.bas to your Agon Light 2's SD card. Then:

    LOAD "datecalc.bas"
    RUN

You'll see a menu with four options. Press 1, 2, 3, or 4 and
press Enter to choose.


OPTION 1: DAYS BETWEEN DATES
-----------------------------

This tells you how many days separate any two dates.

How it works:
  1. Enter the first date (year, month, day) when prompted.
  2. Enter the second date.
  3. Choose a calculation method:
       A = Actual calendar days (real number of days)
       B = 30/360 bond basis (every month = 30 days,
           every year = 360 days -- used in finance)

The program shows you the result and writes it to datecalc.log.


OPTION 2: ADD DAYS TO A DATE
-----------------------------

What date is 90 days from today? This option finds out.

How it works:
  1. Enter a starting date.
  2. Enter the number of days to add (use a negative number
     to subtract days, like -14 for "two weeks before").
  3. The program shows the resulting date and its weekday.

Example: May 7, 2026 + 5 days = 2026-05-12, Tuesday


OPTION 3: DAY INFO
-------------------

Get details about any date.

How it works:
  1. Enter a date.
  2. The program shows:
       - The date itself
       - The weekday (Monday, Tuesday, etc.)
       - The day of the year (1 to 365/366)

Example: May 7, 2026 is a Thursday, day 127 of the year.


OPTION 4: QUIT
--------------

Exits the program.


DATES YOU CAN ENTER
-------------------

The program works with dates from the year 1900 through 2099.
All dates are validated -- if you enter February 30, it will
ask you to try again.

The program understands leap years correctly:
  - 2024 was a leap year (divisible by 4, not by 100)
  - 2000 was a leap year (divisible by 400)
  - 1900 was NOT a leap year (divisible by 100 but not 400)


LOG FILE
--------

Every time you use options 1, 2, or 3, the results are saved
to a file called datecalc.log on your SD card. The file shows:

  === Days Between Dates ===
  Date 1: 2026-05-07
  Date 2: 2026-05-12
  Basis: Actual
  Result: 5 days
  ---

You can view this file on your Agon Light or on a PC to keep
a record of your calculations.


ABOUT THE PROGRAM
-----------------

Inspired by: HP-12C financial calculator
Written for: Agon Light 2 microcomputer
Language:    BBC BASIC V for Z80
Calendar:    Gregorian, with leap years correctly handled
